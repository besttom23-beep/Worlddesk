Sources, access & how the briefing works +

This is an independent, noncommercial reading desk. Headlines link to the original publishers; no full articles are copied. Topic labels are automatically assigned and may occasionally overlap.

The published briefing is refreshed by a GitHub workflow every six hours once hosting is enabled. This page checks for a newer edition every 15 minutes. “Refresh” checks the published file; it does not contact publishers directly.

Reuters and Bloomberg links lead to their public YouTube videos. Their article subscriptions are not included. Xinhua is China’s state news agency; DW and France 24 are public international broadcasters. Perspectives and editorial standards differ.

Listed videos, lectures and selected readings are intended for free access. Optional donations, paid certificates and publishers’ premium archives are excluded. YouTube, PBS and other services can restrict content by country, network, age or embedding settings; an original-source link is always available. A successful URL check cannot guarantee playback everywhere.
